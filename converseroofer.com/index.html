Why it matters now

Hail damage doesn't leak on day one. It leaks in February.

A golf-ball hailstone hitting a shingle at 60+ mph knocks the protective granules loose and bruises the asphalt mat underneath. The roof looks fine from the driveway. Six months of Texas sun later, those bruises crack, and you get the leak in the middle of a cold front.

  • Insurance policies have filing deadlines. Most Texas policies want the claim within a year of the storm, and some are shorter.
  • Adjusters look for a pattern of fresh hits. The longer you wait, the harder the damage is to tie to the September 11 storm.
  • A free inspection now gives you a dated photo record either way.

Questions homeowners ask

Hail damage FAQ

My roof looks fine from the ground. Should I still get it checked?

Yes. Hail bruising on asphalt shingles is almost impossible to see from the driveway. The tell-tale signs are on the roof itself: granule loss, soft spots in the mat, dented vents and dinged gutter lips. Golf-ball hail at 60 mph wind can damage a roof that looks perfect from below.

Will filing a claim raise my rates?

In Texas, insurers generally can't surcharge you for a single weather-related claim that wasn't your fault. Rates across the whole area rise after big storms whether you file or not. Ask your agent to confirm how your specific policy handles "act of God" claims.

Can you cover my deductible?

No, and any roofer who offers to is breaking Texas law (it's been a criminal offense since 2019). Your insurer can require proof you paid it. We'll help you understand your deductible up front so there are no surprises.

How fast can you get out here?

Usually within a day or two in Converse, Kirby, Windcrest, Universal City and Live Oak. If you have an active leak, call and we'll tarp it first.

What if the adjuster says there's no damage?

You have the right to a re-inspection, and we'll meet the second adjuster with our photo report. If your carrier still won't budge, the Texas Department of Insurance consumer line is 800-252-3439 and we can walk you through appraisal options.
